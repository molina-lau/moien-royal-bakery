How we started this company

"Our biggest challenge has come from a customer perspective. If I could go back to March 2020, I think the key thing I’d do differently would be to engage with our customers earlier on, letting them know we were there for them. Typically, our relationships with customers have been onsite, with high-touch engagement, making sure everything is working how they need it to be. Doing everything virtually has made this much harder, meaning we’ve had to get smarter at managing our customer relationships. The virtual aspect has created a longer process for winning new business, but we’ve actually managed to grow our existing customers.
